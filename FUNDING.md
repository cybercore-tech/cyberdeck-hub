# Funding & Infrastructure Support

Thank you for considering supporting this project! 

To be completely transparent, any financial support received through sponsorships 
or donations is **not-for-profit**. Contributions are not a source of income; they 
are used directly to cover the operational overhead and infrastructure costs 
required to keep the project's services running.

### Where the Funds Go

Donations go toward paying for active recurring operational services, including:

- **Linode Server Space:** Hosting backend infrastructure and project documentation.

- **Cloudflare:** DNS management, security, and CDN services.

- **Google Drive & Storage:** Project asset storage and backup archives.

- **Email & Domain Services:** Professional communication infrastructure.

- **System Maintenance & Upgrades**

Every contribution directly offsets out-of-pocket maintenance costs and keeps the lights on. Your support is genuinely appreciated.
